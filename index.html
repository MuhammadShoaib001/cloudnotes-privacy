Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how CloudNotes ("we", "our", or "the app") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our mobile application. By using CloudNotes, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information to provide and improve our services:

  • Account Information: When you sign in using Email/Password or Google Sign-In, we collect your name, email address, and profile picture (if provided by Google).
  • User Content: Notes, titles, messages, images, and other content you create or upload within the app. Images you upload (including past papers) are stored using Cloudinary, our image hosting provider.
  • Usage Data: Information such as likes, interactions, and activity within the app (e.g., liking a Community Note).
  • Device Information: Basic technical information such as device type and app version, used for troubleshooting and performance purposes.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To let you create, save, edit, and share notes
  • To enable community features such as public notes and likes
  • To let you upload and share past papers and other images within the app
  • To authenticate you securely via Firebase Authentication and Google Sign-In
  • To improve app performance, fix bugs, and enhance user experience

3. Data Storage and Security

CloudNotes uses Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, and Firebase Storage) to securely store and manage your data, and Cloudinary to host images you upload within the app. Both providers apply industry-standard security practices, including encryption in transit, to protect your information. Access to your private notes is restricted to your own account through Firebase Security Rules.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. Information may only be shared in the following circumstances:

  • With Google Firebase, as our backend service provider, solely to operate the app
  • With Cloudinary, as our image hosting provider, solely to store and deliver images you upload (e.g., past papers)
  • Content you choose to make public (e.g., posting in Community Notes, or past papers uploaded to a shared university/subject list) will be visible to other users of the app
  • If required by law, legal process, or to protect the rights and safety of our users

5. Third-Party Services

CloudNotes uses the following third-party service providers, which have their own privacy policies governing the use of your information:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Storage) — firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google Sign-In — policies.google.com/privacy
  • Cloudinary (image hosting for uploaded images and past papers) — cloudinary.com/privacy

6. Your Rights and Choices

  • You can edit or delete your notes at any time within the app
  • You can delete public posts you have created
  • You may request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us using the details below

7. Children's Privacy

CloudNotes is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that a child has provided us with personal information, we will take steps to delete such information.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
